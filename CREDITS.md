## 3D models shown in screenshots

The screenshots and the demo video show my own MakerHub library. About half of the 3D models in it are my own designs, including the two opened in the demo: the planchette tealight holder and the cat coaster. The rest were designed by other people and downloaded from public model platforms. Those appear only as content inside the app and belong to their creators. This repository does not include or redistribute any model files.

If you designed a model that appears here and would rather it didn't, open an issue and I will replace the image.
